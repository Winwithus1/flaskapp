WinWithUs Manual

Follow these steps to get started

Steps to Participate:

  • Step 1: Register an account by providing your name and email.
  • Step 2: Contribute to the lottery pool to enter the lottery. Contributions are mandatory for participation.
  • Step 3: Once entered, you'll automatically be included in future drawings.
  • Step 4: Check the date for the next lottery drawing and make sure you're ready!
  • Step 5: After each drawing, winners will be notified via email.

Lottery Rules:

  • Contributions can be made at any time before the drawing.
  • You can only win once per drawing, even if multiple entries are made.
  • All contributions are non-refundable.
  • Winners will be selected at random during each drawing.
